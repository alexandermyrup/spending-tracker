What's different from V1 and V2:

  • Single bar per month instead of paired bars — income is the container (outlined), spending fills it from the bottom
  • You instantly see how much of your income was consumed by spending
  • Over-budget: spending fill turns red above the budget marker
  • Budget marker extends slightly beyond bar edges for visibility
  • More compact — one bar takes less horizontal space than two, so months breathe more
  • Trade-off: harder to read exact income amount (it's the outline height, not a solid fill)
